01About the event

Imagine.Innovate.Inspire.

Project Expo 2026 is organised by the three departmental associations of SINCET — ARINDASCI (AI&DS), the Association of Computer Engineers (CSE) and INFONEX (IT) — bringing the Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, Computer Science & Engineering, and Information Technology together under one roof.

The expo encourages innovation, interdisciplinary collaboration, technical knowledge sharing, and practical applications of emerging technologies.

Participation guidelines.

Prepare to explain your idea clearly and demonstrate how it can make a difference.

  1. 01

    Projects must relate to one of the expo's ten project domains.

  2. 02

    Clearly explain the problem statement and your proposed solution.

  3. 03

    Demonstrate technical knowledge, creativity, and practical application.

  4. 04

    Original work and responsible use of technology are encouraged.

  5. 05

    Explain the tools and technologies used in your project.

07Need to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers for future innovators across AI&DS, CSE and IT.

When and where is Project Expo 2026?

Project Expo 2026 is on Friday, 13 November 2026 at SINCET, Pappakovil, Nagapattinam, Tamil Nadu. The exact start time will be announced by the organisers.

Which departments and events are part of the expo?

IDEAFEST — Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, ARINDASCI Association.
FUTUREVERSE — Department of Computer Science & Engineering, Association of Computer Engineers.
INNOVATEFEST — Department of Information Technology, INFONEX Association.

Which project domains are available?

Ten expo-wide tracks: AI & Machine Learning, Data Science & Analytics, IoT & Embedded Systems, Robotics & Automation, Agriculture/Healthcare/Biotechnology, Smart Cities & Sustainability, Cybersecurity & Networking, Software & Intelligent Applications, Business Analytics, and Game Development & AR/VR. The same tracks are shown across the three department views; confirm department fit with your coordinator.

Who can participate?

Students from engineering, polytechnic and arts & science colleges are invited. Eligible streams include engineering disciplines, diploma programmes, and relevant arts & science programmes. See the eligibility section for the listed disciplines.

What is the team size and registration fee?

Teams are 2–3 students. Registration is ₹100 per head for internal participants and ₹200 per head for external participants. Food is provided for external participants only. Cash prizes are to be won; prize and certificate details will be announced by the organisers.

How do I register?

Choose your department in the Departments section first, then use the registration form (opens in a new tab) linked through the Register buttons. A Google sign-in may be required. This page currently links one verified form. Department-specific forms have not been confirmed; if your department publishes one, use the link shared by its coordinators.

Who can I contact for official updates?

Contact the faculty and student coordinators listed in the coordinators section for your department. The listed phone numbers are the official event coordination contacts from the 2026 poster.
